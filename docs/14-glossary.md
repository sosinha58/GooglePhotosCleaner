> Part of the PhotoTidy PRD set. Index: `README.md`. Rules for agents: `AGENTS.md`.

## 18. Glossary
- **Exact duplicate:** byte-identical files.
- **High-visual duplicate:** visually identical with same dimensions, determined without access to bytes.
- **Near duplicate:** same photo after resize, recompression, crop or small edit, or from a burst.
- **Keeper:** the item recommended to remain in a group.
- **Takeout:** Google's official export tool for user data.
- **pHash:** perceptual hash resistant to resizing and mild edits.
- **Embedding:** numeric vector from an image model; similar images have similar vectors.
- **ADR:** Architecture Decision Record.
- **Dry run:** full flow without applying changes.
